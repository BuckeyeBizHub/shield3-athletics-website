Layer 1 · Surface

System 3 SDP

The Shield3 SurfaceBond surface treatment. Bonds onto surfaces to form a persistent antimicrobial barrier that stays active between routine cleanings. Available in 1-gallon, 32 oz, and 16 oz.

Safety Data Sheet available on request.

Layer 2 · Storage Air

Storage Defense Sachets

Chlorine dioxide (ClO₂) sachets for continuous atmospheric treatment in enclosed storage — gear bags, lockers, and equipment rooms. Available in Bag, Room, and Space sizes.

Private-labeled via Beckart Environmental.

Bundle

Athlete Protection Kit

A starter bundle for individual athletes and teams: a 16 oz surface spray plus three Bag-size Storage Defense Sachets. Built for personal gear between practices.

Great for team orders.

Accessories

Holders & Accessories

Branded sachet holders for wall, ceiling, locker, and bag-clip mounting — so storage-air treatment stays exactly where it's needed.

Cross-sells with Storage Defense Sachets.
