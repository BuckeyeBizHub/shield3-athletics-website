The problem

The protection gap

Locker rooms, mats, shared gear, equipment storage — high-contact, high-moisture spaces in constant turnover, used by one young athlete after another.

Programs clean diligently. But conventional disinfectants only protect while they're wet. The moment they dry, protection stops — and the surface is open again to the next practice, the next shared bench, the next piece of equipment.

The issue isn't too little cleaning. It's that nothing is protecting those surfaces in the long hours between cleanings.

Protection between two cleanings — illustrative

The solution

Three layers. Total protection.

Contamination travels three ways in athletic environments — across the surfaces athletes touch, through the air inside storage, and on the gear they carry home. Shield3 covers all three, and every layer works alongside your cleaning.

01

Layer 1 · Surface

Surface Barrier

Protects the benches, mats, handles, and lockers athletes touch most.

Bonds to high-touch surfaces and keeps working after it dries — so the protection stays put between routine cleanings instead of wiping away with the next pass.

02

Layer 2 · Storage Air

Storage Defense

Treats the air inside lockers, gear bags, and equipment rooms.

Continuous air treatment for enclosed storage — exactly where humidity and shared gear concentrate the problem and the air sits still between uses.

03
