It's a fast, standard way to gauge how clean a surface is. In the field, we take an RLU reading before treatment and again after, on the same marked zones, to document the change in surface bioburden under real conditions. We keep this field evidence separate from our laboratory chemistry validation, and we report ATP results as bioburden measurements — not as pathogen-specific kill claims. **CTA:** See the proof we hold today → --- ## Pricing — "Facility treatments are quoted to the space" Facility treatment programs are scoped and priced to your square footage, surfaces, and schedule.
