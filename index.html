The problem

The protection gap

Locker rooms, mats, and shared gear are high-contact, high-moisture spaces — used by one young athlete after another, all day long.

Programs clean diligently. But the moment a cleaning is done, those surfaces are open again — and nothing is protecting them until someone cleans the next time.
