The problem

The protection gap

Locker rooms, mats, and shared gear are high-contact, high-moisture spaces — used by one young athlete after another, all day long.

Programs clean diligently. But the moment a cleaning is done, those surfaces are exposed again — with nothing guarding them until the next pass.

The system

One system. Three layers of defense.

Contamination travels three ways in athletic spaces — across surfaces, through stored air, and on gear. Shield3 answers all three.
