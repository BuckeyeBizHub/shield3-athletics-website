The problem

The protection gap

Locker rooms, mats, shared gear, equipment storage — these are high-contact, high-moisture spaces in constant turnover.

Programs clean diligently. But conventional disinfectants only protect while they're wet. The moment they dry, protection stops — and the surface is open again to the next athlete, the next practice, the next shared piece of equipment.

The issue isn't too little cleaning. It's that there's nothing protecting those surfaces in the hours between cleanings.

Protection between two cleanings — illustrative

The solution

Three layers. Total protection.

One protective system across the three places contamination travels in athletic environments — the surfaces athletes touch, the air inside storage, and the gear they carry. The layers work together, and all of it works alongside your cleaning.

01 Surface

Surface Barrier

A protective barrier that bonds to high-touch surfaces — benches, mats, handles, lockers — and keeps working after it dries. Unlike conventional disinfectants, it doesn't wash away with the next wipe; it stays active between routine cleanings.

02 Storage Air

Storage Defense

Continuous air treatment for enclosed storage — lockers, gear bags, and equipment rooms — where humidity and shared gear concentrate the problem most. Protection where the air sits still and the gear piles up.

In development
03 Gear

Gear Defense

Protection that carries into the wash, extending the barrier to gear and uniforms between uses. In active development — not yet a shipping product.
